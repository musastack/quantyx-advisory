## Appendix — When to Add Paid Ads

Once organic is generating discovery calls and you have 1–2 clients:

1. **LinkedIn Sponsored Content** — boost your best-performing organic posts (£10–20/day, very targeted)
2. **Google Search** — target keywords like "business reporting dashboard UK", "connect Xero to dashboard", "management reporting automation" (£500–1,000/mo starting budget)
3. **Retargeting** — retarget website visitors via LinkedIn or Meta (low cost, high relevance)

Hold off on paid until you have:
- A confirmed working contact form + Calendly
- At least one real case study
- A clear, tested message (organic will tell you what resonates)
